Privacy Policy

This policy describes Audio Underlay's current pre-release/TestFlight beta. We aim to keep the app straightforward: your audio belongs to you, and most of the app's playback data stays on your device.

Effective date: October 18, 2026
Contact: audiounderlay.support@gmail.com

1. Information the app uses

Imported audio: If you import audio using Files, Audio Underlay copies the selected file and related track metadata to its local app storage so it can play and organize the audio. We do not run a developer-operated server that receives your imported songs.

Music Library: If you grant access, Audio Underlay uses Apple's on-device media-library APIs to display and play eligible tracks and their metadata. You can manage this permission in iOS Settings. The app does not require access to your entire library to play audio you import separately.

App preferences: Settings such as repeat behavior, library selections, playlists, queue information, volume preferences, and widget presets may be stored locally on your device (including app-group storage shared with Audio Underlay's widget extension).

2. Accounts, payments, and advertising

Audio Underlay does not require you to create an Audio Underlay account. The initial beta presents Premium as Coming Soon: no live Premium checkout is offered, and no production ads are requested or displayed by the beta's Release configuration.

We use Apple's StoreKit infrastructure for local development tests and entitlement handling. If payment functionality becomes available in a future release, Apple will handle the purchase transaction. We will update this policy and the in-app disclosures before enabling live purchases.

Third-party advertising SDK: The app project includes the Google Mobile Ads SDK for development testing. The beta Release code does not initialize or request display ads. Google documents that its SDK can process information such as device identifiers, network information and diagnostic or interaction data when used. Before activating production ads, we will review the SDK's privacy manifest, implement applicable consent mechanisms, and update this policy and App Store privacy disclosures. More information: Google Mobile Ads SDK data disclosure.

3. Support email and beta feedback

If you email audiounderlay.support@gmail.com, you choose which information to send, such as your email address, device details, screenshots, and descriptions of issues. We use this information to respond, investigate reported problems, and improve the app. Messages are handled through Google's Gmail service and may be retained as necessary for support and follow-up, subject to applicable requirements. Please avoid sending sensitive information or copyrighted audio files.

TestFlight and iOS may also provide diagnostics and feedback under Apple's own services and your device settings. Apple's handling of those records is described in Apple's Privacy Policy.

4. Storage, sharing, and security

Imported audio and app preferences are intended to remain in local app storage. Some device data may be included in backups according to your iOS/iCloud settings. We do not sell imported audio or support correspondence. Support messages are handled by our email provider, and Apple and Google may process data under their services as described above.

No system is perfectly secure. Keep your device updated and avoid sending personal secrets in support requests.

5. Your choices

  • Decline or revoke Music Library access through iOS Settings.
  • Remove individual imported tracks within the app where supported, or remove app data by uninstalling Audio Underlay. Device backups may remain subject to Apple's backup settings.
  • Request information about, or deletion of, support correspondence by emailing audiounderlay.support@gmail.com. We will review requests consistent with applicable requirements.
  • Adjust TestFlight and iOS diagnostic-sharing settings using Apple's controls.

6. Children

Audio Underlay is a general-purpose audio playback app, not designed specifically for children. We do not knowingly solicit personal information from children through our support service.

7. Policy changes

If we introduce additional features, production advertising, or paid services, we may revise this page to reflect the practices in the current version of the app. The effective date above will be updated when material changes are published.

8. Contact

For privacy requests or questions, contact audiounderlay.support@gmail.com.
