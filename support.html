Quick answers

Why does playback stop when I open another app?

Audio mixing depends on iOS audio-session behavior and the other app. Calls, Siri, and some apps may interrupt playback. Return to Audio Underlay and try resuming.

Which songs work from my Music Library?

Audio Underlay supports eligible, locally accessible, unprotected tracks. Cloud-only or protected media might not be available for underlay playback. You can also import compatible audio files using the Add tab.

Where are my imported files stored?

Imported audio is copied into the app's local storage on your device. Removing the app or its data can remove those files, so keep your own backup of important originals.

Can I purchase Premium during the beta?

No. Premium is marked Coming Soon. Proposed plans and benefits are previews and may change. Beta Release checkout is disabled.

How do I give feedback?

Email the support address above with your idea or issue. You can also use TestFlight's built-in feedback tools when the beta is available.
